Enterprise hiring has a new vocabulary problem. In a single quarter, TA leaders have been pitched AI copilots, AI recruiters, agentic ATS platforms and autonomous hiring. Most of these pitches lead with the model: which LLM, how many parameters, how fast it writes a job description, how it goes about assessment and so on.
